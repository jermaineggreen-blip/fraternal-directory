Vouch for a member

Is this person a current member in good standing of your organization?

  • Vouch only if you personally know they are a current member in good standing.
  • You are confirming membership only, not the quality of their work or business.
  • Your name stays private. Their listing shows “Verified (member referral).”
  • You can vouch for up to 5 members per year.
  • If someone you vouch for is found not to be a member, you lose the ability to vouch and FVD may review your listing.
