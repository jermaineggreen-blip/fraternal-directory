Privacy Policy

Last updated November 14, 2026

What we collect

When you submit a listing we collect: your name, email, and phone; your organization, chapter or lodge name, and membership number; your city and state; your military or veteran status, if you choose to provide it (optional); your occupation, years in the field, and bio; and, if you list a business, its name, contact details, address or service area, website, and any logo or photos you upload.

What is public

Public listings show your name, organization, chapter, occupation, location, and bio. Business Listings also show the business details you entered. Only the last four digits of your membership number are ever shown, and your personal phone and email are not published.

What stays private

Your personal phone, personal email, and full membership number are used only to review and verify your listing, contact you about it, and process your annual fee. We use your membership details to confirm membership, and we may share your name and membership details with an officer of your organization for that purpose. We do not sell your information.

Who processes it

Submissions are stored in Airtable and handled through Zapier and Gmail. Payments, when enabled, are handled by Stripe; we do not see or store your card number.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to correct or remove your listing and your stored information. Email jermaineggreen@gmail.com to make the request.

Children

The directory is for adult members only.
