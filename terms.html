Terms of Use

Version 2026-11-27 · Last updated November 27, 2026

1. What this is

The Fraternal Vocational Directory is an independent, member-run directory where members of fraternal, Masonic, and sorority organizations list their professions and businesses so other members can find and support them. It is not operated by, and has not been endorsed by, any fraternal organization unless that organization says so in writing.

2. Who may list

You may list only if you are a member of the organization(s) you name, and the information you submit must be true. Submissions are reviewed before they go live. We may ask an officer of your organization to confirm your membership, and we may decline or remove any listing.

3. Fees and contributions

Founding members who sign up on or before March 31, 2027 receive any listing type (Member, Business or Business Elite) free for one year from the date their listing is approved, and no fees are collected during that year. Before any fees begin, we will publish pricing on this site and give listed members at least 30 days’ notice by email, and you may remove your listing at any time. You are never charged without first agreeing to a fee. A share of future listing fees will support participating organizations once approved by organization leadership; details will be published before any fees are collected.

3a. Membership verification

Every listing is reviewed before it goes live. You may upload a photo of your current dues card to help us verify your membership; it is optional, kept private, and deleted once you are verified. Listings submitted without a dues card may be subject to further verification, which can include contacting you or an officer of your organization. Instead of a dues card, a member of your organization who was verified with a dues card may vouch for you. A vouch confirms membership only; it is not an endorsement or review of anyone's work, and the voucher's name is kept private. Vouched listings show “Verified (member referral).” Each member may vouch for up to five members per year, and we may remove the vouching privilege, and review the voucher's own listing, if a vouched member is found not to be a member. We may decline or remove any listing whose membership we cannot confirm.

4. What we publish

Member Listing: your name, organization, chapter, occupation, city and state, and the short bio you write. Business Listing: all of the above plus the business name, business phone, business email, website, and business address or service area you provide, and any logo or photo you upload. Your personal phone number, personal email, and full membership number are not shown publicly. Visitors can send you a private message or quote request through the directory; it is forwarded to you by email, and your contact details are not shown to the sender.

5. No endorsement

Listings are provided by the members themselves. Neither the directory nor any fraternal organization endorses, guarantees, or is responsible for any member's business, work, or advice. Do your own research before hiring or paying anyone, and settle any dispute directly with that member.

6. Your responsibilities

Keep your information accurate and current. Do not post anything unlawful, misleading, or that uses an organization's name, emblem, or logo in a way the organization has not permitted. Organization names may appear on listings so members can identify their own membership; this does not mean the organization has endorsed or partnered with the directory. The directory uses an organization’s emblem only with that organization’s written approval or as a partner. If you are a verified member in good standing, you may display your own organization’s name, letters or emblem on your own listing. By checking the box at sign-up, you confirm that you are a member in good standing and have the right to display your organization’s marks. Every listing states that it was listed by the member and that the organization has not endorsed or partnered with the directory, unless that organization has agreed in writing.

6a. Reports and removal

Every listing has a “Report this listing” button. If an organization objects to the use of its name, letters, emblem or any other content on a listing, we will remove that content within 48 hours of receiving the objection at fraternalvocationaldirectory@gmail.com. We may also place the listing on hold while we review it. We keep a record of each report and what we did about it.

7. Removal and changes

You may ask us at any time to edit or remove your listing. We may update these Terms; continued use after an update means you accept it. These Terms are governed by the laws of the State of Georgia.
