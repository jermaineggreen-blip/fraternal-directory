Frequently Asked Questions

Fraternal Vocational Directory · Version 2026-11-27

Who can be listed?

Members of fraternal, Masonic, and sorority organizations. Your membership is confirmed before your listing goes live.

How do people contact me?

Business and Business Elite listings show Call, Email, Website and Request a Quote buttons, and quote requests are emailed straight to you. Free member listings show your trade, city and organization. When someone wants to hire you, we email you to let you know, and you can open a Business listing to receive their request. Your personal email address and phone number are never shown on the site.

What does it cost?

Nothing right now. Founding members who sign up by March 31, 2027 get any listing type (Member, Business or Business Elite) free for one full year from the day they are approved. Before any fees begin, we will publish pricing and give you at least 30 days’ notice, and you can remove your listing at any time.

Where does the money go?

Listing fees keep the directory running. A share of future listing fees will support our organizations; details will be shared once they are approved by leadership. Founding members who join by March 31, 2027 are free for one year after approval.

Do I have to upload my dues card?

No, it is optional, but it speeds up verification. Your dues card photo is kept private, seen only by the reviewer, and deleted as soon as you are verified. Listings without a dues card may need extra verification and may be removed if membership cannot be confirmed.

Can another member vouch for me?

Yes. If you don't have your dues card handy, a member of your organization who was verified with a dues card can vouch for you. We email them privately, and they tap Yes or No. A vouch confirms membership only. Your voucher's name stays private, and your listing shows “Verified (member referral).”

What is shown publicly?

Your name, organization, chapter, occupation, city and state, and your bio. Business Listings also show the business details you entered. Your personal phone, personal email, and full membership number are never shown.

How long does approval take?

Usually 2 to 3 business days. You will receive an email at each step.

Can I get a refund?

Fees are non-refundable once paid, so please review your listing before you pay.

How do I change or remove my listing?

Email fraternalvocationaldirectory@gmail.com with your name and the change you want, or ask for it to be removed. Removal requests are handled promptly.

Does my organization endorse the businesses listed?

No. Listings are submitted by members themselves. No organization endorses or guarantees any member's business or services.

Can I show my organization's letters or emblem?

Yes, on your own listing, if you are a verified member in good standing. You confirm this with a checkbox when you sign up. Each listing notes that it was listed by the member and is not endorsed by the organization.

How do I report a listing?

Open the listing and tap “Report this listing,” or write to fraternalvocationaldirectory@gmail.com. If an organization objects to content on a listing, we remove it within 48 hours.

I think a listing is wrong or misleading.

Email the address above with the listing name and what is wrong, and it will be reviewed.
